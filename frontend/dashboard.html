📘 Phase 2 Guide (click to expand)

How Phase 2 works

Phase 2 uses your library WebVPN session to access papers through institutional subscriptions:

  1. URL Conversion — DOI → publisher URL → WebVPN proxy URL
  2. PDF Extraction — Parse the publisher page for PDF links
  3. Download — Fetch PDF through WebVPN with your credentials

PKU Library WebVPN

北大图书馆 — wpn.pku.edu.cn, wrdvpn path-style proxy (hostname AES-encrypted in the URL path). Downloads run requests-first for speed, with an automatic browser retry for JavaScript-heavy pages.

Troubleshooting

  • "No cookies configured" — Click Step 1 and complete PKU login in the Chrome window
  • "Session expired" — Re-login (Step 1) to refresh cookies
  • "Access denied" — The journal may not be in PKU library's subscription
  • All downloads fail — Try Sci-Hub (Phase 1) again, or manually download
